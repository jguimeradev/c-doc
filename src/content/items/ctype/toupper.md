---
name: "toupper"
kind: fn
header: ctype
summary: "Converts a lower-case letter to upper case."
signature: "int toupper(int c);"
source: https://sourceware.org/glibc/manual/latest/html_node/Case-Conversion.html
---

Returns the upper-case equivalent of `c` if it is a lower-case letter; otherwise `c`. See [`tolower`](../fn.tolower/).
